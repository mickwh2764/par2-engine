HOW TO READ THIS

Each spring is one tissue. Spring radius = |λ| = how strongly that tissue's transcriptome oscillates circadianly. Larger spring = higher temporal persistence.

The Z-axis is time — one full revolution = 24 h. All springs normally turn together in phase (a synchronised bundle).

Jet lag twists the outer springs relative to the inner ones. The SCN (innermost, yellow) re-aligns first. Your lung is still misaligned days later — the largest spring untwists slowest.
