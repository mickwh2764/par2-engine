HOW TO READ THIS

Orbital radius = τc, temporal memory. Tighter orbit = faster reset. Wider = longer re-entrainment lag.

The SCN (hypothalamus) sits innermost — it resets in ≈2.6 h, adapting to a new time zone in ~1 day. Your lung orbits widest — 8.8 h memory — so it re-aligns days after your brain has settled.
